1. Overview & Architectural Privacy Commitment

EdgeCue ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is designed from the ground up as a privacy-first, confidential, fully on-device AI cognitive rehearsal and speech teleprompter tool for interview preparation and practice.

Our engineering architecture is air-gapped by design, governed by three uncompromised guarantees:

  • Zero Cloud Transmission: All audio capture, voice activity detection (Silero VAD), acoustic speech-to-text recognition (Sherpa-ONNX Zipformer), question classification, and large language model inference (Google LiteRT-LM Gemma) execute 100% locally on your device hardware (Neural Processing Unit / EdgeTPU, mobile GPU, or CPU). No audio, spoken words, questions, or answers ever leave your device.
  • Zero Voice or Biometric Retention: Audio captured via your device's microphone is held ephemerally in volatile system RAM only while speech is active. Raw audio is immediately discarded and overwritten upon utterance endpointing. We never record, persist, serialize, or transmit audio files or acoustic voiceprints to disk or any remote server.
  • Zero Developer Telemetry or Tracking: The Application contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising libraries, and zero cloud tracking beacons. We do not track, collect, monetize, sell, or transmit personal data, interview questions, transcripts, or usage patterns.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Handled

A. Microphone Audio (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

Microphone audio is accessed solely during active, user-initiated practice sessions to perform real-time speech-to-text transcription and detect spoken interview questions.

  • RAM-Only Ephemeral Lifecycle: Audio chunks (16 kHz 16-bit mono PCM float arrays) are processed in-memory through a circular ring buffer and streamed into local neural networks. Trailing audio buffers are flushed and released immediately when speech pauses. Audio is never written to persistent flash storage or external media.
  • Foreground Service Transparency: Continuous audio capture operates strictly under Android's FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE with an ongoing system notification and an accessible, one-tap "Stop Assistant" action button. Microphone capture terminates immediately when stopped.
  • Legal Consent & Wiretapping Notice: Before microphone access is requested, the Application presents a mandatory disclosure requiring users to acknowledge compliance with applicable local, state, and international wiretapping and recording consent laws (such as all-party consent statutes).

B. Local Session History & Transcripts

Practice transcripts and generated cue talking points are stored exclusively on your device in a private, sandboxed Android Room/SQLite database (interview_assistant.db) within internal app-specific storage (/data/data/com.edgecue.app/databases/).

Cloud Backup Exclusion: EdgeCue configures strict Android Auto Backup exclusion rules (res/xml/backup_rules.xml and res/xml/data_extraction_rules.xml). All databases, preferences, and session transcripts are explicitly excluded from Google Drive cloud backups and device-to-device transfers, ensuring your sensitive interview practice never syncs to third-party cloud storage.

C. On-Device AI Model Weights Provisioning

To enable offline, zero-latency inference, the Application allows you to download open-weight neural model files (Silero VAD, Sherpa-ONNX Zipformer, and Google LiteRT Gemma).

  • Direct Encrypted CDN Downloads: Model assets are retrieved directly over encrypted HTTPS from Hugging Face (huggingface.co / hf.co). EdgeCue does not route transfers through intermediate proxy servers and collects no connection metadata.
  • Integrity Verification: All downloads are verified locally via streaming SHA-256 cryptographic checksums and exact byte length checks before use.
  • Full Offline Functionality: Once models are downloaded, EdgeCue operates completely in Airplane Mode without internet connectivity.

D. User-Initiated Support & Feedback (Optional)

The Application includes an optional "Send Feedback" feature in Settings. Feedback is never transmitted automatically or in the background.

When initiated, the Application composes a plain-text/Markdown template and launches your device's native email client via Intent.ACTION_SEND addressed to hbenitez@hectisai.com. You have complete transparency and control to review, edit, or delete any content before deciding to send.

You may optionally choose to attach diagnostic metadata (app version, hardware SoC tier, model verification status) and/or recent session transcripts by explicitly checking in-app opt-in boxes. These options are disabled (unchecked) by default. Any feedback submitted is used strictly by the publisher to resolve technical defects and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

3. Data Sovereignty & User Deletion Rights

You maintain absolute ownership and control over all local data generated by the Application:

  • Clear Interview History: Navigate to the Activity tab (or Settings) and select "Clear History" to permanently and irreversibly purge all stored interview Q&A records and latency telemetry from the local database.
  • Purge AI Model Assets: Navigate to System & Diagnostics and select "Purge Downloaded AI Models" to instantly delete all local neural weights (~3.1 GB) from your device flash storage without uninstalling the app.
  • Application Uninstallation: Uninstalling EdgeCue via Android OS settings immediately and irreversibly destroys all sandboxed databases, preferences, cache files, and downloaded model weights.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

EdgeCue is professional interview preparation software intended solely for adults aged 18 and older preparing for job, collegiate, or technical interviews. We do not knowingly target, solicit, or collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable by law).

5. Application Security & Defenses

  • Global HTTPS Enforcement: The Application enforces a strict Android Network Security Configuration (res/xml/network_security_config.xml) that globally blocks all unencrypted cleartext HTTP traffic.
  • Zero Dynamic Code Execution: EdgeCue loads only passive numerical neural weights (.onnx and .litertlm) into verified native runtimes. It never utilizes dynamic class loading (DexClassLoader) or downloads executable binaries.
  • Production Log Stripping: All verbose, debug, and informational logs are stripped at compilation time via ProGuard/R8 to prevent transcript or audio telemetry leakage to Android system logcat.
