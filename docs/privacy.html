1. Overview & Core Architectural Guarantee

EdgeCue ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is designed from the ground up as a privacy-first, confidential, fully on-device AI cognitive rehearsal and speech teleprompter tool for interview preparation and practice.

Unlike cloud copilots that upload live microphone streams and sensitive candidate resumes to remote servers, EdgeCue executes its entire machine learning and generative reasoning stack locally on your Android handset. Our architectural commitment is governed by three uncompromised guarantees:

🔒 Zero Cloud Transmission

Speech recognition, voice activity detection, and language model inference execute 100% locally on your device hardware. No spoken words, questions, or answers ever leave your device.

🎙️ Zero Voice/Biometric Retention

Audio is buffered ephemerally in volatile system RAM only while speech is active and is discarded immediately after acoustic processing. We never store, serialize, or transmit audio recordings.

🛡️ Zero Developer Telemetry

We do not track, collect, monetize, sell, or transmit personal data, interview questions, transcripts, usage analytics, or telemetry. No third-party tracking or advertising SDKs exist in the app.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Handled

A. Microphone Audio (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

Microphone audio is accessed solely during active, user-initiated practice sessions to perform real-time speech-to-text transcription and detect spoken interview questions.

  • RAM-Only Ephemeral Lifecycle: Spoken audio is digitized into 16 kHz 16-bit mono PCM float arrays in volatile RAM, processed through a zero-allocation circular ring buffer (CircularFloatRingBuffer), fed to the on-device Silero VAD and Sherpa-ONNX Zipformer neural network, and immediately overwritten in memory upon utterance endpointing. Audio is never written to persistent flash storage, cache directories, or external SD cards.
  • Foreground Service Notice: Continuous audio capture operates strictly under Android's FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE with an ongoing system notification in the notification shade featuring an accessible, single-tap "Stop Assistant" action button that terminates microphone capture immediately.
  • Two-Party Wiretapping & Consent Notice: Before microphone access is requested, the Application presents a mandatory disclosure requiring users to acknowledge compliance with applicable local, state, and international wiretapping and recording consent laws (such as California Penal Code § 632, Florida, Illinois, Massachusetts, and international all-party consent statutes).

B. Local Session History & Transcripts

Practice transcripts and generated cue talking points are stored exclusively on your device in a private, sandboxed Android Room/SQLite database (interview_assistant.db) within internal app-specific storage (/data/data/com.edgecue.app/databases/).

Cloud Backup Exclusion: EdgeCue configures strict Android Auto Backup exclusion rules (res/xml/backup_rules.xml and res/xml/data_extraction_rules.xml). All databases, preferences, and session transcripts are explicitly excluded from Google Drive cloud backups and device-to-device transfers, ensuring your confidential interview practice never syncs to third-party cloud storage.

C. Floating Teleprompter HUD & Focus Modes (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

EdgeCue provides an optional floating teleprompter overlay (FloatingHudOverlay) that hovers over video conferencing apps (Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack) or coding documents during practice sessions.

  • Stealth OLED Low-Glare Display: Designed in obsidian midnight black (#06070A) to eliminate eyeglass glare reflections on webcams and minimize ocular fatigue during extended 60–90 minute interview loops.
  • Minimized Mascot Pill: Features the Dachshund mascot icon and "EdgeCue" status label in a compact 48dp accessible pill.
  • Interview Focus Modes: Directs generative cues across distinct interview disciplines (System Design, Algorithms & Coding with Big-O complexity, Behavioral STAR method, QA / Code Review, and General Leadership).
  • Content Reporting & Safety Notice: Displays a persistent AI hallucination notice and includes an in-app "Report" action (ReportAiContentDialog) allowing users to flag inaccurate or biased responses locally.

D. On-Device AI Model Weights Provisioning (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC)

To enable offline, zero-latency inference, the Application allows you to download open-weight neural model files (Silero VAD, Sherpa-ONNX Zipformer, and Google LiteRT Gemma 4).

  • Direct Encrypted CDN Downloads: Model assets (~2.6 GB to 3.1 GB) are retrieved directly over encrypted HTTPS from Hugging Face (huggingface.co / hf.co). EdgeCue does not route transfers through intermediate proxy servers and collects no connection metadata.
  • Cryptographic Checksum Verification: All downloads are verified locally via streaming SHA-256 cryptographic checksums and exact byte length checks before being marked ready for inference.
  • Full Offline / Airplane Mode: Once models are downloaded, EdgeCue operates completely in Airplane Mode with zero internet connectivity.

E. User-Initiated Support & Feedback (Optional)

The Application includes an optional "Send Feedback" feature in Settings. Feedback is never transmitted automatically or in the background.

When initiated, the Application composes a plain-text/Markdown template and launches your device's native email client via Intent.ACTION_SEND addressed to hbenitez@hectisai.com. You have complete transparency and control to review, edit, or delete any content before deciding to send.

You may optionally choose to attach diagnostic metadata (app version, hardware SoC tier, model verification status) and/or recent session transcripts by explicitly checking in-app opt-in boxes. These options are disabled (unchecked) by default. Any feedback submitted is used strictly by the publisher to resolve technical defects and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

3. Data Sovereignty & User Deletion Rights

You maintain absolute ownership and control over all local data generated by the Application:

  • Clear Interview History: Navigate to the Activity tab (or Settings) and select "Clear History" to permanently and irreversibly purge all stored interview Q&A records and latency telemetry from the local database.
  • Purge AI Model Assets: Navigate to System & Diagnostics and select "Purge Downloaded AI Models" to instantly delete all local neural weights (~3.1 GB) from your device flash storage without uninstalling the app.
  • Application Uninstallation: Uninstalling EdgeCue via Android OS settings immediately and irreversibly destroys all sandboxed databases, preferences, cache files, and downloaded model weights.

4. Hardware Acceleration & Runtime Environment

EdgeCue is optimized for flagship and mid-tier Android devices running Android 13 to Android 17 (API 33–37):

  • Google Tensor EdgeTPU / NPU (Pixel 8 / 9 / 10 Pro): Direct southbound native acceleration via bundled libLiteRtDispatch_GoogleTensor.so shims for sub-300ms Time-To-First-Token (TTFT).
  • Qualcomm Snapdragon & Samsung Exynos: OpenCL and Vulkan mobile GPU acceleration with automatic dynamic sequence caching.
  • Thermal & Memory Protection: Automatic thermal status monitoring adaptively caps token generation during thermal surges, and onTrimMemory hooks release cache memory to prevent system memory pressure.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

EdgeCue is professional interview preparation software intended solely for adults aged 18 and older preparing for job, collegiate, or technical interviews. We do not knowingly target, solicit, or collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable by law).

6. Application Security & Defenses

  • Global HTTPS Enforcement: The Application enforces a strict Android Network Security Configuration (res/xml/network_security_config.xml) that globally blocks all unencrypted cleartext HTTP traffic.
  • Zero Dynamic Code Execution: EdgeCue loads only passive numerical neural weights (.onnx and .litertlm) into verified native runtimes. It never utilizes dynamic class loading (DexClassLoader) or downloads executable binaries.
  • Production Log Stripping: All verbose, debug, and informational logs are stripped at compilation time via ProGuard/R8 to prevent transcript or audio telemetry leakage to Android system logcat.
