## 3. Full Description (Character Limit: Max 4,000 Characters)

```markdown
Ace your technical, system design, and behavioral interviews with EdgeCue — the world’s first 100% local, on-device AI teleprompter and real-time cognitive coaching assistant.

EdgeCue listens via state-of-the-art streaming speech recognition, detects interviewer questions as they are spoken, and instantly displays concise, structured architecture prompts and STAR bullet points — completely offline with ZERO cloud egress.

🔒 100% PRIVATE & AIR-GAPPED
Your interview audio and career conversations belong to you alone. EdgeCue executes full-stack neural speech-to-text (Sherpa-ONNX streaming Zipformer) and large language model inference (LiteRT-LM Gemma) entirely on your handset's NPU, GPU, or CPU. 
• Zero audio or transcript data ever leaves your device.
• Functions seamlessly in full Airplane Mode.
• No cloud servers, no subscriptions, no accounts, and no data telemetry.

⚡ REAL-TIME COGNITIVE TELEPROMPTER
Never lose your train of thought under high-pressure interview conditions:
• Live Audio Question Detection: Neural acoustic VAD and Zipformer transducer accurately capture English questions in real time.
• Instant Prompts & Architecture Cues: Sub-15ms Time-To-First-Token (TTFT) on Google Tensor EdgeTPU hardware generates immediate bulleted frameworks.
• Floating HUD Overlay: A draggable, resizable, translucent floating pill that sits above video call apps (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams) without obstructing your view.
• Stealth OLED Design: Ultra-low glare dark canvas (#06070A) engineered to eliminate eyeglass reflections on webcams and reduce eye fatigue.

🎯 SPECIALIZED INTERVIEW FOCUS MODES
Filter cues dynamically across distinct interview disciplines:
• System Design: Scalability patterns, partitioning, caching strategies, replication, and distributed consensus.
• Algorithms & Data Structures: Time/space complexity trade-offs, edge cases, sliding windows, and dynamic programming heuristics.
• Behavioral (STAR): Structured Situation, Task, Action, and Result framing to articulate impact clearly.
• QA & Code Review: Edge-case auditing, test isolation, static analysis, and regression prevention.
• General Leadership: Communication clarity, stakeholder management, and team conflict resolution.

🐾 THE DACHSHUND MASCOT: CALM, LOYAL, EVER-ATTENTIVE
EdgeCue's minimalist Dachshund mascot serves as your quiet companion during high-stakes calls. Its alert posture and droop ear symbolize continuous, faithful listening, helping reduce candidate anxiety while keeping answers structured and confident.

⚙️ OPTIMIZED FOR FLAGSHIP HARDWARE
• Google Tensor EdgeTPU (Pixel 8 / 9 / 10 Pro): Native hardware acceleration via libLiteRtDispatch shims for lightning-fast inference.
• Qualcomm Snapdragon & Samsung Exynos: OpenCL/Vulkan GPU acceleration with automatic dynamic sequence caching.
• Thermal & Memory Throttling Protection: Real-time battery and thermal monitoring prevents device overheating during marathon multi-round interview loops.

📋 ETHICAL USE & CANDIDATE TRANSPARENCY
EdgeCue is an educational and cognitive teleprompter designed to assist candidates with recall, structured articulation, and anxiety reduction. EdgeCue does not fabricate qualifications; it empowers prepared candidates to communicate their genuine experience at their absolute best.
```

### Data Safety Form Declarations (Google Play Console)
- **Data Collection:** **No data collected.** The app does not collect, transmit, share, or store personal or audio data off-device.
- **Data Sharing:** **No data shared with third parties.**
- **Permissions:**
  - `RECORD_AUDIO`: Required solely for real-time local microphone speech-to-text processing. Audio frames are processed in volatile memory and never saved to persistent storage.
  - `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE`: Required to maintain uninterrupted local speech recognition while the teleprompter overlay is displayed during video calls.
  - `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`: Required for the floating teleprompter HUD overlay window.
